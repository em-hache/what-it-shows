The two rows add up to 412, the number merged into #4471. Five of the 379 read as one-day shifts rather than month flips. They are counted in ticket #1 and need a check against the fix.

A fix for the parser does not fix the booking-v3 form. The form tells tenants to type MM/DD, so a parser fix alone leaves 33 reports open.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges a report by its subject line. All of these reports use one of about a dozen subjects, such as "Wrong day on the invite", so they merged into #4471 whatever their cause. Intake's tag is a guess: it tagged 15 of the 33 form reports as parser.
